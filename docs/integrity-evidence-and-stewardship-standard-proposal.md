## Proposed normative topics

1. **Claim classification:** distinguish sourced facts, observations, interpretations, inferences, hypotheses, estimates, and speculation in controlled artifacts.
2. **Evidence provenance:** preserve source identity, version/time/context, relevant transformation, and evidence artifact for consequential claims.
3. **Uncertainty and calibration:** communicate material limitations and avoid confidence beyond evidence quality.
4. **Verification honesty:** never claim a test, review, approval, deployment, or verification occurred without a recorded result.
5. **Falsification:** high-impact changes include disconfirming evidence, counterexamples, boundary cases, and failure hypotheses.
6. **Correction integrity:** material corrections preserve the prior state, reason for change, supporting evidence, and downstream impact.
7. **Risk-proportionate review:** review depth scales with consequence, uncertainty, novelty, and reversibility.
8. **Responsible power:** capability does not imply authorization; least privilege, approval, audit, and recovery controls remain mandatory.
9. **Non-collapsing evaluation:** integrity and critical safety controls are gates, not tradeable points in a single aggregate score.
10. **Stewardship:** high-impact decisions disclose affected parties, long-term consequences, trade-offs, and escalation routes.

## Candidate acceptance evidence

- Standards review approves exact language and scope.

- Automated checks validate what can be checked mechanically; human review covers judgment-dependent controls.
